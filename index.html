Visual Alankars

One phrase per line. S R G M P D N. Lowercase r g d n are komal, M# is tivra Ma. S' is the upper octave, N. the lower.
Space plays or pauses, or tap the stage. H hides the controls. When hidden, tap the top left corner to bring them back.
